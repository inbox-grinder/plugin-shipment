# Fixture privacy

Published fixtures use synthetic identities and sample message content. Do not add real emails, account numbers, payment details, booking references, tracking identifiers, credentials, or delivery message IDs. Public service addresses and API origins describe supported integrations.

The initial migration reviewed source, manifests, templates, documentation, fixtures, and lockfiles. Billing identifiers and delivery metadata were replaced with synthetic values. Shipment fixtures use a synthetic USPS tracking identifier. Build outputs and the app repository history are excluded.
